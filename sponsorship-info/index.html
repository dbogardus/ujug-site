Tax advantages

Sponsoring SALT puts your company's name and logo in front of thousands of Utah software professionals, so sponsorships are generally deductible as an ordinary and necessary business expense (marketing and advertising) under IRC §162.

This is general information, not tax advice. Please consult your tax advisor about your specific situation.
